First-time setup

What is this PC?

Every PC in the centre runs this same copy. Tell this one what it is; you can change it later from the launcher.

Which pod is this?

The console lists its pods by number and name, and the pod shows its name under Ready.

e.g. Pod 1, or a name of your own

Its screens

Click the monitor the pilot looks through - the main view, where Ready shows - then the one with the cockpit screens (the MFDs, the radar and the plasma sign in Firestorm; the radar in 3.0). Both games are set up for them, full screen.

Its controls

The stick, throttle and pedals as Windows sees them. Move them and press their buttons: what moves lights up here. Fine-tuning the bindings is on the launcher's Controls pages.

The network

Windows asks for an administrator's permission once.
