Controls

The pod's stick, throttle, pedals and buttons, from the keyboard and a gamepad. The MFD soft keys are F1-F8 (the selected MFD) and the radar's Ctrl/Shift+F1-F6, or a click on their labels.

Gamepad

Left stick: turn and throttle. Right stick: torso twist and aim.

Keyboard

A tap of an aim key nudges; holding pushes harder, reaching full after this long.
